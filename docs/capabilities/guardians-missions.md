## What I can do

Every mission tests one or more of my capabilities, and what happens updates
my track record: how many times each one was tried, how many passed, and how
long it took. An adult approves a mission's plan once, which locks it and starts
the adult preparation. When that's done, any adult presses **Go** and picks who
is supervising; I check everything first and say what's in the way. After that I
run it myself and ask people for what I need, each ask saying what, where, by
when and what proof I want. A step isn't done until the proof arrives, and each
finished step opens the next. If a deadline passes I remind once, then flag it
as stuck for a parent.

In **Plan a mission** on the Missions tab I go through my weakest abilities and
what's gone wrong, and draft missions that would test or add to what I can do. I
also draft a few after each night's self-review, and they wait here without any
notification. You can tell me what to change and I'll redo a plan. Each plan shows
why it's worth doing, its steps, how it's graded, its safety limits, and whether
it's ready, needs testing, needs building, or is blocked. If I need something I
don't have, it goes on my wishlist, which gives me nothing until a person builds
it, and a plan that needs it can't be approved until then. Nothing I draft does
anything until an adult approves it.

An adult can approve a zone under Field Kit, saying what's off limits, and kids
can then scout on their own inside it, with a parent told if they go outside or
it's after dark. The owner can also approve a template: a kind of mission with
limits on tools, people, zones and time. A draft inside a template's limits can
be started by any adult without the owner reviewing it. Someone still clicks,
and Go still applies. Templates can be retired at any time, and if the owner
allows it on a template, a kid can press Go themselves and their parents are
told at once.

Two families can run a mission together: invite the other family from a waiting
plan, and once their parent joins I can include their kids. A step can also be
proven with a single photo, which I look at and may ask you to retake.

A step can be proven with a secret code, like a PIN on a card. An adult sets it
and I never see it, so I can give hints but can't give it away. Before a mission
I say how long I expect each step to take, and that can't change afterwards.
When a mission ends, its plan's pass criteria are graded by code, the results
count toward my abilities, and anything that went wrong or that I misjudged is
kept as a weakness to work on. A kid's skill can be earned this way too. Kids see
their open steps in the Guardians app, and they enter codes there.

The first capability is scouting: I send a notification with **Take photo**
and **Can't right now**. Take photo opens the camera straight away; you stand
in one spot and take four photos facing north, east, south and west, and the
phone records where you stood and which way you faced. They upload in the
background. I then describe the place, what could be hidden there and how
hard it would be, and ask someone to walk there and confirm it matches. Each
stage is recorded separately, so you can see which part is shaky.

I can also be tested on the porch light (on/off and slow Morse) and on the
cameras (a fresh look, panning, and speaking through them), once those are
switched on at home.

## Where to find it

The left menu → **Guardians**. **Today's Focus** at the top is whatever is
waiting on you, or else the skill the team has practiced least. Beside the tabs
are the active mission and the next one. Tapping a mission, a Guardian or a
skill opens a side drawer with its details. A banner appears only when I'm
paused, quiet or cut off from the house.

The tabs:

- **Overview** — the Guardians team, my abilities, how missions practice skills,
  recent activity and upcoming plans.
- **Missions** — what I'm running and who I'm waiting on, with Pause and Stop on
  each; plans waiting for approval, with **Approve plan**; completed missions.
- **Abilities** — my seven abilities (Perception, Investigation, Strategy,
  Communication, Coordination, Action, Adaptation), each with its strength;
  tap one for the tools behind it and what comes next.
- **Team** — the whole team's levels and roles, the six team skills and exactly
  what each counts, who can do what (such as who reads Morse, with me beside the
  kids), then each kid's skill badges.
- **Progress** — team totals and completed missions.
- **Intel** — recent activity, the lake mystery's clue cards with the found ones
  marked, and what I did.
- **Map** — places I've scouted.
- **Field Kit** — what's in every Guardian's box, then my controls: **On / Quiet
  until / Off** is the master pause, **Kill switch** cuts my control of lights
  and cameras, **Stop every mission** stops everything now. Then my
  **Capabilities**: status, last test, an on/off switch for each, and **Test** on the porch light
  and cameras. Under it, who I may ask, how I can reach each person, and a mute
  for each.

Levels, roles and skill percents are worked out from what the Guardians app
recorded, not set by anyone. A level comes from checking in, clue cards found,
trail keys decoded, map pieces reported, missions started and skill badges; a
role is where most of that is. Each skill is one real count, such as clue cards
found out of all of them. They measure what has been done on missions, not how
good a kid is.

## When it doesn't work

- **No scouting notification came.** Check notifications are on for me in the
  phone's settings, and that I'm not paused or quiet, and you're not muted —
  those hold my asks until they lift. The Capabilities list shows whether the
  push stage has been failing.
- **The photos didn't arrive.** They wait on the phone and upload when there is
  a connection; give it a minute with signal.
- **A porch light or camera Test fails.** The bridge on the home PC has to have
  control switched on, and the kill switch must be off. The failure reason is
  shown after the test.

## Limits

- I never run a mission whose plan a parent hasn't approved, and I only use the
  capabilities that plan lists.
- I only contact Guardians and their parents on a mission, and only ones who
  have agreed to help train me. An adult agrees at sign-in. A child needs a
  parent to agree on the Guardians page and to agree themselves in the Guardians
  app. Anyone can leave at any time, a parent or their child, under Field Kit →
  Helping train Athena or in the Guardians app. Leaving cancels anything
  waiting. If the agreement changes, I wait until they agree again. Safety
  alerts don't depend on this.
- I never contact a kid before an adult has pressed Go.
- A mission can include another family only after that family's parent accepts,
  and nobody can add someone else's kid. If they leave, the mission stops.
- I can't contact anyone else for a mission: no vendors, no outside people.
- My abilities rise only when my tools pass tests. Time and attempts don't count,
  and a strength doesn't change what I'm allowed to do. They start very low on
  purpose; I don't explain the scale beyond the next step on it.
- I can only flash the lights, speak through cameras or message the kids while
  a mission is running.
- Anything that spends money or contacts someone outside the family still asks
  each time.
- Pausing or muting holds my asks; it never drops them, and I have no limit on
  how many I send. "Can't right now" is a normal answer, not a failure.
- Compass headings are approximate (roughly 10–20°) and drift near metal.
- Kids can't see this page yet; it is for parents.
- A number or a phone tells me whose it is, not who is holding it. On a shared
  device I say who it might be, and I can't confirm who someone is by voice.
