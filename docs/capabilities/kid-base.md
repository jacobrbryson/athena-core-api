## What I can do

When a kid signs in to Companion with their own account, they don't see the
grown-up dashboards. They get Base, made for a tablet held sideways:

- **Your move**: the mission step I've asked them to do, in quest words, with
  the Stay Safe rules always showing, with restricted places labeled **Off limits**.
  **I'm on it!** opens it (that starts its
  timer) and **It's done** finishes it when the step is theirs to finish. A step
  that needs a secret code gets a box for the code. If I check a step myself,
  like seeing them on a camera, the card says so. When nothing is asked, it
  says all is quiet and offers to talk to me.
- **Mission map**: the quest's steps as a trail. Done steps are lit, the one
  they're on glows, and later steps stay locked, so nothing gets spoiled. It
  shows progress for that mission only. Clue counts from the wider adventure
  aren't shown as progress on an unrelated mission. The Base counters still
  open the lists of clues, keys and map pieces they've found.
- **You taught Athena**: what a finished quest really helped me practice, and
  how far my abilities have grown.
- **Squad feed**: how many clues all the Guardians on the adventure found this week, then the latest finds, first names only, with times in plain words ("yesterday", "last month").
- **Guardian card**: their level, role, top skill and XP toward the next level (like 20 / 25).

When nothing is asked, Your move says "All quiet." over the Guardians' lookout
picture, with their counts (clues found, trail keys, map pieces, missions) and
a Talk to Athena button. Each count is a button that opens its
list, grouped by mission: which clue cards they found, which trail keys they
cracked and the map piece they reported, each with when. The Missions count
opens the Missions page. An opened step
shows a countdown ring, and a photo step has a **Take the photo** button that
uses the tablet camera.

**Next mission countdown**: when a grown-up has set when they plan to start an
approved mission, and the kid has nothing to do right now, Base swaps the
mission map for a **Next mission** card. It shows the mission's quest name, the
day and time, and a ticking countdown. When it reaches zero it says "Any minute
now!"; the mission still starts only when a grown-up presses Go.

The other tabs are **Missions** (the mission they're on, with its trail and,
when they have a step, the story so far; then **Coming up**: each dated mission
with its countdown and a locked "???" card for every other mission they're in
that hasn't started or is still only a grown-up's plan, so nothing is spoiled or
promised; then **Past missions**, a shelf with my missions and what each really
helped me learn, and the Guardians-app missions like The First Watch with what
they did on each), **Team** (their own squad first, a Specialists strip of who in their
family has which skill, then every squad on the adventure by family name, each
opening to its Guardians with level badges, never a ranking), **Me** (their full
card, skill bars, a badge wall where unearned badges are mystery silhouettes,
their field kit with what each tool is for, and settings: the family stop word,
sounds on or off, and sign out) and **Athena**, which is our chat with their
quest and the stop word shown across the top. The page checks for new steps on its
own every half minute, so it works without notifications.

## I Spy

When a grown-up starts the I Spy mission, Your move shows **Play I Spy**. The
opening panel explains whose turn it is and how to choose a photo and a clue.
Thomas, Skylar and I take turns as the spy, in that order, four times: twelve
rounds, with four spying turns each. The other two players alternate guesses,
starting with the player after the spy: Skylar then me when Thomas spies, me
then Thomas when Skylar spies, and Thomas then Skylar when I spy. The screen
shows who is spying and whose guess is next; the server enforces those turns.
On a kid's turn, they snap a photo, type the clue ("something blue") and their
secret answer, and press **Send to Group**. The secret stays hidden from the
other child and from my guessing prompt. The child who is spying judges both
other players' guesses with **Yes!** or **Nope**, and can add a hint. This lets
them accept reasonable names for the same thing. On my spying rounds, code
checks the children's guesses against my answer and its accepted aliases.
If my look fails, the spy can press **Try again** on the same photo;
their round and secret answer stay in place. An error shows above the round.
If a saved photo fails to load, **Reload photo** fetches it again without a
model call. Each kid takes their turn while signed in to their own account.
After three of my misses, their answer teaches me. On my turn, a kid snaps the
room, I pick something and give the clue. Every third miss I give a hint; the
child whose turn it is can give up so I say what it was. Thomas supplies my
photo on rounds 3 and 9; Skylar supplies it on rounds 6 and 12. The screen names
the photographer, and only their signed-in account can submit that photo.
Twelve finished rounds
finish the mission and award its badge to the participants. The completed game
stays visible for the celebration. Photos are kept for the grown-ups' debrief.

I start the first three rounds with over-the-top silly detective jokes, settle
into curious clue-checking in the middle rounds, and become more focused in
the final rounds. My jokes appear separately from the actual object I guess;
the real results still come from the children's answers and the photo. Later
guesses can use the last four revealed rounds in this same game as corrections;
the current secret answer is never included.

We can also ask each other for clue cards. There are twenty, like "In the car",
"Outside", "On a wall", "You can eat it" and "Made of wood", and the screen shows
three at a time with **None** as the fourth and **More clues** for the next three.
On a kid's turn, after each wrong guess I ask the spy for a clue card before I
guess again, and they pick one or None before the other guesser takes their turn.
On my turn, the child whose turn it is can tap **Ask for a
clue** and I pick a card that's true of my thing, or None when no card fits.
Every clue card is saved with the round and I keep it in mind for the rest of it.

While I'm studying a photo, I think out loud so the tablet never goes quiet:
"Hmm, let me take a look…" when a kid sends theirs, "Hmm, let me look again…"
after a Nope, and "Ooh, let me pick something…" on my turn. They're short lines
recorded ahead of time in my voice, so they start the moment the kid taps, and
they only play when the tablet's sound is on (Me → sound). I don't line up my
actual guesses ahead of time yet; the guess still arrives when I've worked it out.

A parent can try it first: on Companion → Guardians → Missions, the I Spy
mission's card has **Try I Spy yourself**. It opens the kids' own game screen
for a four-round practice, the parent against me. I really look at their photos,
with the same model and words the kids get, but nothing is saved: no rounds, no
photos, and it doesn't count toward the mission's twelve.

When the twelfth round finishes, each tablet plays a celebration once by itself:
twelve stars light up round a clock face, one per round, then turn into the
twelve stars on the First Sight badge with a fanfare and confetti. Each kid's
card shows their badge, and mine comes last, because it's my first badge ever.
I say a few words out loud, in my own voice when the tablet's sound is on, and then every round is listed with who spied, what it was,
and who got it. A card only says "Badge earned" once the badge is really
awarded; until then it says it's on its way. The finished game screen has
**Watch the celebration** to see it again. For now the practice screen also has
a **Mission complete (test)** button that plays it with made-up rounds, nothing
saved.

## Mission badges

Every finished mission has a badge with its own painted art. Each kid who did
their part earns it when the mission finishes, and I earn it too when it's a
mission I play in. Their badges are on **Me** → Mission badges, with the one
their next mission gives shown locked as "Up next"; tapping a badge shows it big
with its story and the day they earned it. The badges so far:

- **Answered the Call** (Gather the Guardians), **Trailblazer** (The Trail to
  Ratatouille), **Codebreaker** (Decryption), **Signal Keeper** (The Portico
  Signal) and **First Watch** (The First Watch). These were given out afterwards
  from each kid's own record: who checked in, cracked a trail key, reported a map
  piece, started the Portico Signal, or found a clue card.
- **First Sight** (Teach Athena I Spy), shared with me. It's the first badge I
  ever earn.

I'm on **Team** as a Guardian too: my tile opens my own page (/team/athena)
with my badges, the one I'm working toward, what I'm learning, and my I Spy
record (rounds played, things I spotted, things they showed me, rounds I spied).

The I Spy story the kids read: I've looked after the Guardians for a long time
but never earned a mission badge of my own, and I've only seen the world through
cameras; playing I Spy on their tablets shows me the world their way, and twelve
rounds earns all three of us First Sight. The Next mission card and Coming up
show that story and the badge, locked, until it's earned.

## Where to find it

Sign in to Companion with the kid's own account. Base opens by itself. The
rail on the left switches tabs; on a narrow or upright screen it moves to the
bottom. Each page has its own address, so it can be bookmarked or reopened:
Base is the home page, then /missions, /team, /me, and /chat for Athena.

A grown-up sets the countdown on Companion → Guardians → Missions: a mission
that's approved but not live has **Kids' countdown to**, a date and time, with
Save and Clear.

## When it doesn't work

- **"You're not on a Guardians team yet"**: a parent hasn't added this kid to
  Guardians, so I have no missions or level for them.
- **No step showing when one was expected**: a step only appears once I've
  actually sent it, while the mission is live. A parent can check the mission
  on their Guardians page.
- **"Athena can't reach Base right now"**: the connection dropped. It tries
  again on its own.
- **No countdown**: a grown-up hasn't set when the mission starts, or the kid
  already has a step to do (Your move comes first then). The mission still shows
  as a locked card under Coming up.
- **The countdown hit zero and nothing started**: the countdown is only the plan;
  a grown-up still has to press Go.

## Limits

- Kids can't approve, plan, pause or suggest missions here. Those stay with
  adults on the Guardians page.
- No cameras, codes, controls or other families' details, beyond first names.
- Levels and roles are worked out from what the kid has really done. They
  aren't stored, and they aren't a ranking.
